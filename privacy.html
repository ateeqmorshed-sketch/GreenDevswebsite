Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Last updated October 6, 2026

Green Devs Ventures Inc., incorporated in Alberta, Canada, in 2026, operates greendevs.ca. This policy explains information handling for this studio website and messages sent to our contact address.

Information involved

  • Contact emails: If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if provided, and the message and attachments you choose to send. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Website requests: Our hosting provider, Vercel, may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, requested URL, and request time to deliver and secure the site.

This website has no account registration, checkout, or contact form. Its current code does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies or local storage for tracking. Gameplay previews are hosted video files, not embedded social media players. Hosting infrastructure may process information needed for security and reliable delivery.

How information is used

Contact information is used to respond to your questions, provide requested support, and handle business inquiries. Technical information is used to serve the website, investigate errors, and protect it against abuse. We do not use this website to build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

Service providers and disclosure

Website hosting and email service providers process information as needed to deliver their services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to address unlawful activity or protect rights and safety. Provider processing may take place outside Canada, where local laws may apply. Read Vercel’s Privacy Policy for its practices.

Retention and security

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the inquiry, maintain necessary business records, or meet legal obligations. Hosting logs are subject to the provider’s retention practices. We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can browse without sending us personal details. To ask about information we hold, request access or a correction, request deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern, email info@greendevs.ca with the subject “Privacy request.” We may need to verify your identity. Legal obligations may limit some requests; withdrawing information needed for support may prevent us from completing that support.

Games, stores, and children

This policy covers the studio website, not data handling inside individual games or third-party stores. Please consult the privacy disclosures provided with the particular game and store before downloading or playing. If you need a game’s privacy information, contact us and name the game.

The website does not provide child accounts or ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us to request its review and deletion where appropriate.

Changes and contact

Updates will be posted here with a revised date. Direct privacy questions to Green Devs Ventures Inc. at info@greendevs.ca. You may also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta about privacy concerns.
